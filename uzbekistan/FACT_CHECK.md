## Confirmed as briefed

- **2025 arrivals:** 11.7M, +3.7M, +46.8% (stat.uz, Jan 2026, preliminary).
- **Top three origin countries:** Kyrgyzstan 3.32M, Tajikistan 2.70M and Kazakhstan 2.67M, which is 74% of all arrivals.
- **April 2025:** the first month above 1,000,000 foreign tourists (Uzbektourism, 7 May 2025).
- **Jan–Feb 2026:** 1,776,336 arrivals, +33% (stat.uz).
- **Arrivals 2010–2019:** World Bank API (ST.INT.ARVL). 2016 = 2.03M, which matches "around two million".
- **Arrivals 2020–2023:** UN Tourism, cross-checked against stat.uz (2022: 5.2M) and press reports (2021: 1.9M; 2023: 6.6M).
- **2024 arrivals:** 8.0M, the base implied by the 2025 release. It was first published as 8.2M.
- **Doubly landlocked:** only Uzbekistan and Liechtenstein (WorldAtlas, Wharton, Wikipedia).
- **Registan madrasas:**
  - Ulugh Beg: 1417–1420
  - Sher-Dor: 1619–1636
  - Tilya-Kori: 1646–1660 (finished after its patron died)
- **Timur:** made Samarkand his capital in 1370 (Britannica; Met Museum).
- **Ulugh Beg's sidereal year:** 365 d 6 h 10 m 8 s, +58 s off the modern value (Krisciunas et al., arXiv 1804.08352). MacTutor quotes a different (tropical) value, so the card is labelled "SIDEREAL YEAR".
- **Bukhara (UNESCO WHC 602):** more than 2,000 years old; Genghis Khan's sack in 1220. The story that the minaret was spared is labelled LEGEND.
- **Itchan Kala (WHC 543):**
  - inscribed in 1990; 650 × 400 m; walls up to 10 m high
  - first World Heritage site in Central Asia: the next earliest are Bukhara (1993), Merv (1999), Yasawi (2003), Sulaiman-Too (2009) and Sarazm (2010)
  - "about fifteen minutes on foot" is the narration's rounding
- **Political and economic dates:**
  - Mirziyoyev elected 4 Dec 2016
  - som made freely convertible 5 Sep 2017
  - 45 countries visa-free from 1 Feb 2019 (Decree UP-5611)
  - France visa-free from 5 Oct 2018; Germany from 15 Jan 2019
  - China visa-free for 30 days from 1 Jun 2025
- **Afrosiyob openings:** Tashkent–Samarkand 8 Oct 2011; Bukhara extension 25 Aug 2016.
- **Plov:** "Palov culture and tradition" inscribed by UNESCO on 1 Dec 2016 (decision 11.COM 10.B.35).
- **Mountains:** Mount Adelung (4,301 m) is the highest point of Tashkent region, in the Pskem range of the western Tian Shan. Amirsoy/Chimgan is about 80 km and 1.5 h from Tashkent.

## Remaining caveats

- **Arrivals are trips, not unique people.** 67% of 2025 arrivals were visiting relatives. The narration already makes this point ("Many of them are neighbors…").
- **Visa map is not exhaustive.** It shows only the documented additions. The counter shows the official total of about 90 countries, so the map never claims more precision than the data has.
